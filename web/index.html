krapy

Mirror your Android screen without the lag.

Built for flaky Wi-Fi and phone hotspots. Rust desktop app, tiny Kotlin phone app. Scan a QR to pair. No adb or developer options unless you want the fallback.

GitHub Releases

How it works

  1. On the desktop, run krapy pair and leave it listening.
  2. Open the phone app and scan the QR code on screen.
  3. Mirror starts. If the phone's IP changes, mDNS finds the desktop again on its own.

Why it is fast

  • Hardware H.264 encode on the phone.
  • UDP video with late frames dropped instead of piling up.
  • At most one frame queued on either side.
  • GPU YUV conversion on the desktop.
  • Adaptive bitrate: steps down quickly on loss, ramps up slowly when the link is clean.
