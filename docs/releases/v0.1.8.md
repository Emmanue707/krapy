Sound fixed, the phone goes quiet when sound is on the PC, pause and disconnect in the notification, and the phone controls on the PC.

### Sound

- sound on the PC played an octave too low and broke up. The phone sends stereo, and the PC was playing it as mono at half speed, so its buffer kept overflowing. It now plays stereo at the right speed, resamples cleanly when the PC is not at 48 kHz, waits for a small buffer before starting, and fades in and out on gaps so there are no clicks
- the phone tells the PC its real sample rate and channels
- when sound goes to the PC the phone turns its media volume to 0 and puts it back when you switch sound back to the phone, disconnect, or the app restarts after a crash. If a phone also silences what it sends to the PC when muted, krapy puts the volume back, keeps the sound on both, and says so in the app and on the PC

### Notification

- Pause / Resume, Sound PC / Phone and Disconnect are buttons right on the notification, visible without expanding it
- pause freezes the last frame on the PC and the window title says paused
- the app asks for notification permission before mirroring, and if notifications are off the connected screen says so and links to the setting

### Controls on the PC

- Alt+M (or F1) opens a small menu in the PC window with pause, quality, sound, keep awake, clipboard sync, rotate, disconnect and live stats. Click a row or use the shortcut
- shortcuts: Alt+P pause, Alt+1/2/3 Smooth/Balanced/Sharp, Alt+S sound, Alt+K keep awake, Alt+B clipboard, Alt+R or Ctrl+R rotate, Alt+D disconnect
- the phone and the PC stay in sync: change a setting on either and the other shows it

### Speed and size

- smaller phone send buffers, so a slow Wi-Fi link cannot pile up old frames
- the phone app is about a third of its old size (578 KB to 192 KB in a local build) and the Linux binary is smaller (9.1 MB to 7.0 MB in a local build), with no change in speed

On a loopback test (60 fps 720p clip, send to decoded) the median is about 4 to 5 ms over UDP and TCP. Real glass-to-glass time depends on the phone encoder and the Wi-Fi.

### Limits

- muting the phone uses its media volume. On a phone that also silences the captured sound when muted, krapy keeps sound on both and tells you
- turning the phone screen off while mirroring is still not possible for a normal app
