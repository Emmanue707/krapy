### Enable remote control (Android 13+)

The APK is installed outside the Play Store, so Android blocks accessibility at first. Mirroring works without it.

1. Settings > Apps > krapy.
2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right) > Allow restricted settings, then confirm with your PIN or fingerprint.
3. Settings > Accessibility > krapy > turn on.
