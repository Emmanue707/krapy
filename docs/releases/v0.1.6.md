Fixes the long lag over Wi-Fi and adds one-command USB mirroring.

- the screen could take a minute to change. The phone sent the H.264 setup data (SPS/PPS) only once, as its own frame, and the desktop kept just the newest frame, so that setup data was usually thrown away. Without it the decoder could not show anything ("non-existing PPS 0" in the log) until the setup data happened to get through
- the phone now puts SPS/PPS in front of every keyframe and sends a keyframe every second. The desktop also keeps the last SPS/PPS, adds them to a keyframe that comes without them, and waits for a keyframe instead of decoding broken frames
- frames are decoded in order. If they pile up the desktop jumps to the next keyframe instead of falling behind, and when a frame is lost or fails to decode it asks the phone for a keyframe right away (at most every 300 ms)
- lower decoder delay (FFmpeg low delay, fast mode, one thread), and the window presents frames without waiting for vsync when the GPU allows it
- FFmpeg messages are hidden unless you pass `-v`. With `-v` krapy prints the measured latency every second
- when a session ends the desktop says why: the phone closed the connection, the phone stopped mirroring, a connection error, or nothing from the phone for 3 s
- `krapy connect --usb` (or just `krapy adb`) mirrors over a USB cable: it finds the phone, sets up `adb reverse`, opens krapy on the phone and starts mirroring. Video goes over TCP on this path because adb reverse only carries TCP
- `krapy adb pair <ip:port> <code>` connects to the phone right after pairing when it can find it
- krapy does not need sudo. It now says so if you run it as root
- if your PC gets a new IP address the phone finds it again over mDNS

On a loopback test (60 fps 720p clip, send to decoded) the median is about 4 ms over UDP and over TCP. Real glass-to-glass time depends on the phone encoder and the Wi-Fi.

### USB in short

1. `sudo dnf install android-tools` (Fedora) or `sudo apt install adb`
2. On the phone: Settings > About phone > tap Build number 7 times, then Developer options > USB debugging on
3. Plug in the phone and tap Allow
4. `krapy connect --usb`, then tap Start now on the phone
